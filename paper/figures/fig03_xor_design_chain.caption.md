English caption

A single XOR design across representations. (a) The actual routed Boolean DAG implements $(a\lor b)\land\neg(a\land b)$ and passes all four scalar input vectors against the frozen source. (b) The exported Normal 2DDWave layout occupies 12 gate-level tiles; a bridge denotes geometrically crossing, electrically separate routes. (c) All QCA cell instances are drawn separately by layer: 72 on layer 0, two on layer 1, and five on layer 2 (79 in total). The upper-layer insets are enlarged and do not depict vertical interconnects. Dot positions show cell geometry, not a simulated polarization state. All panels derive from one production run. This representation chain does not establish source-to-device waveform equivalence.
