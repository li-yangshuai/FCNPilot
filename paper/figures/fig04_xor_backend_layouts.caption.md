English caption

Actual gate-level XOR layouts produced by the three exposed backends. Occupied bounding rectangles are 3 by 4, 4 by 6, and 5 by 13 tiles; the corresponding exported devices contain 79, 119, and 214 QCA cell instances. Each panel is fitted independently for legibility, so the displayed physical sizes are not area ratios. Background colors encode the zero-based clock phases exported in each IFCN artifact; only Normal uses a fixed 2DDWave template. Crossings without a junction remain separate routes. Normal and Compact use six-node Boolean decompositions, whereas June inserts buffers; this is an end-to-end flow illustration, not a comparison on an identical DAG. The June panel uses the separately recorded 600-second-budget follow-up run. These examples establish geometry and artifact provenance, not device waveform correctness.
