English caption

Numerical diagnostics for the same Normal XOR run used in Figure 3. (a,b) All 512 Bistable and 3005 retained Coherence output samples are shown without temporal realignment, interpolation, or smoothing. Baseline and accelerated exported traces coincide, which tests implementation agreement rather than agreement with the source truth table. The abscissa is the retained sample index, not a clock-cycle or physical-time estimate. (c) All seven reported cycles of standard energy analysis are retained, including the large energy-error term in the first reported cycle. The energy step is $10^{-17}$ s; the Coherence trace comparison uses $10^{-16}$ s. The error term is a diagnostic field, not a statistical error bar. Neither a time-step study nor steady-state convergence has been established; the plotted energies do not support a power-accuracy or energy-saving claim.
