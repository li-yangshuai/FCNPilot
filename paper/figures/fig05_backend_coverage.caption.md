English caption

Backend coverage on the frozen 30-representation corpus. (a) All 90 planned representation--backend attempts are retained; N, C, and J denote Normal, Compact, and June. Q indicates source-to-routed-DAG Boolean equivalence and matching QCA input/output terminal counts; U indicates an unsupported DAG audit; I indicates a terminal-count mismatch. U/I records both findings, and a dash records incomplete execution. (b) The original layout/mapping execution criterion gives 21, 10, and 24 completions (55/90); adding the independent logic and interface-count checks gives 16, 9, and 23 qualifications (48/90). Among the 55 execution completions, 51 DAG audits establish equivalence and four are unsupported; none establishes nonequivalence. Four mappings lose output terminals, including one of the unsupported cases, so seven distinct completions fail to qualify. (c) On the 16 function representatives fixed in the benchmark manifest before outcome selection, the corresponding qualified counts are 8, 5, and 13. These checks do not establish device-level waveform, timing, or power correctness. The figure describes the archived implementation used in this matrix, before subsequent production guards were added.

中文说明

冻结的 30 个电路表示在三后端上的完整覆盖情况。N、C、J 分别为 Normal、Compact、June；保留全部 90 项计划运行。Q 表示源电路与布线后 DAG 布尔等价，且 QCA 输入/输出端子数一致；U 表示 DAG 审计方法尚不支持；I 表示端子数不一致；U/I 表示两项同时出现；横线为运行未完成。原布局/映射执行完成判据分别为 21、10、24 项，共 55/90；追加逻辑和端口数量资格检查后为 16、9、23 项，共 48/90。55 项中 DAG 等价 51 项、审计不支持 4 项，没有被证明不等价的项；另有 4 项映射丢失输出端子，其中 1 项与不支持审计重叠，因此共 7 项不能取得追加资格。预先固定的 16 个函数代表分别有 8、5、13 项取得资格。此资格不代表器件波形、时序或功耗已验证；图中为历史冻结实现的矩阵，不能视为后续新增生产门槛后的 90 项重跑结果。
