English caption:
Architecture of the Pi-integrated iFCN combinational design agent. (a) The main agent uses four typed tools to inspect capabilities, control persistent jobs, query status, and read hash-verified artifacts. The durable worker schedules the requested dependencies. (b) A Verilog snapshot is normalized and parsed into a DAG, processed by one of three executable placement-and-routing backends, and mapped into QCA cells. Solid arrows denote circuit or artifact dependencies; dashed arrows denote software control. The dashed lower enclosure is a software workflow grouping, not a physical interconnect. Simulation and energy analysis each consume the mapped QCA artifact. Their execution is ordered by the stage runner, but energy analysis does not consume the simulation-comparison result. Source/DAG Boolean equivalence and terminal counts are separate from device waveform validation; numerical engine agreement and exploratory power estimates do not constitute physical signoff.

中文解读：
上半部分是 Pi 主智能体、四个可识别工具和持久作业执行器；四工具分别对应 ifcn_capabilities、ifcn_run、ifcn_status、ifcn_artifact。下半部分是实际的数据依赖：Verilog 到 DAG、三个真实布局布线后端、原生器件映射，再分别读取 QCA 进行仿真器对照和能耗计算。虚线表示软件控制和逻辑分组，不能解读为共享物理总线。虽然执行阶段有顺序，能耗计算实际读取 QCA，不依赖“仿真对照通过”这个文件。图中不宣称器件波形功能已经签核。
