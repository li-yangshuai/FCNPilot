English caption

Three identity levels of the same frozen benchmark snapshot: 30 source representations (17 TOY and 13 MAJ), 29 byte-distinct frozen files, and 16 complete truth-table groups under fixed input/output declaration order. These are corpus descriptions, not successive causal filtering steps. MAJ/xor5R and MAJ/xor5\_r1 form the sole exact-byte duplicate pair; one representative per function group is fixed in the manifest. The grouping does not quotient arbitrary input/output permutations or NPN equivalence, and equivalent alternative representations do not add independent functions. Only frozen copies are edited: the TOY/xor2 module port header is normalized without changing its logic, and TOY/RCA2 is corrected to the declared two-bit addition interface and checked against an independent arithmetic reference on all 32 input patterns. Original TOY/MAJ files are preserved. MAJ/RCA2 and par\_check retain their source logic because a conventional specification is not sufficiently established; MAJ/RCA2 is not treated as functionally matched to the corrected TOY/RCA2. Historical small-compaction and large-fiction area tables are excluded from new efficacy evidence according to the machine-readable eligibility manifest.
